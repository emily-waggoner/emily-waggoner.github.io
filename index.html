About Me

I am an aquatic biogeochemist investigating how microorganisms shape and are shaped by nutrient cycling. My work spans effluent-fed rivers, intertidal zones, river plumes, and the open ocean.

My research explores phosphorus bioavailability, microbial enzymatic activity, and the environmental fate of natural and synthetic phosphorus compounds.

Research

My research examines how microorganisms acquire, transform, and cycle phosphorus across aquatic environments.

Microbial Phosphorus Acquisition

Investigating how microbial enzymes determine access to chemically diverse organic phosphorus compounds.

Coupled Biogeochemical Cycles

Exploring connections between phosphorus acquisition and carbon and nitrogen cycling.

Synthetic Phosphorus Compounds in Aquatic Environments

Examining the distribution, microbial transformation, and environmental fate of synthetic organophosphorus compounds.
