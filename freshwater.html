Investigating nutrient cycling, microbial processes, and environmental contaminants in the Santa Cruz River.

Field Projects

2025–2026

Over the course of the year, I conducted water, soil, and plant tissue sampling at 10 sites along the Santa Cruz River.

These efforts were made possible by our partners (Tumacácori National Historical Park & the Sonoran Institute) and an incredible team at UA.

2024–2025

Over the course of the year, I conducted water and 16S/18S rRNA sampling at 5 sites along the Santa Cruz River, three of which were located in Tumacácori National Historical Park.

These efforts were made possible by our partners (Tumacácori National Historical Park & the Sonoran Institute) and an incredible team at UA.
